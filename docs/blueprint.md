# **App Name**: Susan Fashion

## Core Features:

- Product Display: Display available products with their images, names, and prices on the homepage in a grid layout.
- Product Page: Detailed product pages show the product image, price, sizes (if clothing), available colors, and a description.
- Product Image Upload: Admin can upload images of their products directly from their gallery. A generative AI tool helps improve image quality and automatically resize it to the right specifications.
- Product Management: Admin panel for adding new products with name, price, sizes, colors, and description, and publishing them to the storefront.
- Order Placement: Simple checkout form to collect customer's first name, last name, and phone number for cash on delivery orders.
- Order Notifications: Immediate email notifications to the store owner when an order is placed, containing customer details, product information and time of order.
- Splash Screen: Animated logo and transition to homepage

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Gold (#D4AF37) to evoke luxury and elegance.
- Background color: Off-white (#FAF9F6) for a clean and premium feel.
- Accent color: Dark gray (#333333) to highlight specific elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a serif (body text) matched with 'Belleza', a humanist sans-serif (headings).
- Use minimalistic line icons in gold to maintain the elegant aesthetic.
- Use a clean and minimal grid layout to display products and information.
- Use smooth transitions and subtle animations for a premium user experience.
